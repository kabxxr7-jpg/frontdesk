Try it yourself

Call like a real customer would. A few ideas:

  1. Book a job: "I'd like a leak repair visit next week, in the morning." Give your real email to get the confirmation.
  2. Ask a question: "Do you cover Round Rock?" or "Are you licensed?"
  3. Change your mind: call back, give the code from your email, and move or cancel it.
  4. Try to trip it up: ask for a time when they're closed, or one that's already taken.
  5. Say something urgent, like "smell gas": it stops booking and gets you to a person.

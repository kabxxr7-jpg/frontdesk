Try it yourself

Call like a real patient would. A few ideas:

  1. Book an appointment: "I'd like a new patient exam and cleaning next week, in the morning." Give your real email to get the confirmation.
  2. Ask a question: "Do you take Delta Dental?" or "How much is whitening?"
  3. Change your mind: call back, give the code from your email, and move or cancel it.
  4. Try to trip it up: ask for a time when they're closed, or one that's already taken.
  5. Prefer typing? Open the chat and do the same in text: it books into the same calendar and sends the same email.
  6. Say something urgent, like "knocked out": it stops booking and gets you to a person.
